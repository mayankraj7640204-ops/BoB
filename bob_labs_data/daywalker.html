Budget Fine Art Paper, 170gsm | A1 (59.4 × 84.1 cm / 23.4 × 33.1 in)

A lightweight matte paper with a smooth, understated finish. Colours are clear and faithful to the original, with a softer tonal range and gentler blacks. Ideal for exhibitions, temporary displays, and decorative spaces. Not archival, so we recommend framing behind glass and keeping it out of direct sunlight.

Choose this if: you want the look of the artwork at an accessible price, or you plan to refresh your display over time.


Giclée Fine Art Print, 200gsm | A1 (59.4 × 84.1 cm / 23.4 × 33.1 in)

Printed with fine pigment inks on a heavier, smooth matte stock. Colours are saturated and luminous, blacks are deeper, and gradients flow without banding. Fine detail stays crisp, bringing the print closer to the original work. A refined, durable choice for the home.

Choose this if: you want gallery-quality colour and finish without the premium price.


Hahnemühle German Etching, 310gsm | A0 (84.1 × 118.9 cm / 33.1 × 46.8 in)

Our museum-grade option. Mould-made, acid-free, and archival, this paper has a pronounced, tactile grain that gives the artwork the depth of a traditional etching. Colours are warm and rich with a natural softness, never glossy, and the surface shifts subtly as the light moves across it. Made to last for generations, and at A0 it makes a commanding statement.

Choose this if: you are investing in a centrepiece, value texture and longevity, or want the closest experience to an original fine art piece.

Prints are made to order and leave the studio within 7-10 working days. Framed pieces travel in a corner-protected crate; unframed sheets ship rolled in an acid-free tube. Tracked worldwide.

Shipping is US$27 to the United States, United Kingdom, Canada and European Union, and US$40 to all other destinations, converted to your checkout currency.

Shop prices exclude tax. Applicable sales tax is added at checkout for US delivery addresses only.

Every painting and print in my shop is made to order, so I'm not able to accept returns for a simple change of mind. But I want you to be genuinely happy with your piece, if something arrives less than perfect, I'm happy to arrange a replacement if your order has:

  • Damage that happened during shipping
  • A paint defect
  • A print defect

How to report an issue:
Reach out within 7 days of delivery at support@shutabug.com, with a few clear photos of the issue. That helps me see exactly what happened so I can get a replacement sorted for you as quickly as possible.

Please note: claims made after 7 days, or without photos, may not be eligible for a replacement — so don't wait too long to reach out!
